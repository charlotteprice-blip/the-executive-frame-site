The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Emotional regulation before performance. Access to the prefrontal cortex when the stakes are highest. The physiological foundation without which clarity and intentional action cannot be built. You cannot perform from inside threat activation — and competitive pressure is threat activation by design.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Strategic thinking under pressure. Reading the field, the opponent, the moment. Working memory, inhibition, cognitive flexibility. The cognitive scaffolding that lets capability survive the conditions of competition.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Values-aligned action. Not the perfect move — the deliberate one, taken on purpose, repeated under load. Autonomy in your sport. Competence grounded in evidence. The architecture of a career that compounds across a season.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because you cannot read the field from inside threat activation. Clarify must precede Choose because you cannot execute with intention from cognitive overwhelm. This is biology — and competitive performance runs on the same biology as any other human work.
