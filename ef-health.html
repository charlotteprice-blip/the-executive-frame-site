The approach

Health is never only physical — or only psychological.

Symptoms are real. Health is shaped by the interaction between the body, nervous system, attention, emotion, behaviour, relationships and context. EF Health helps people understand these interactions and build practical ways forward, alongside appropriate medical care.

The Executive Frame™ model

Centre · Clarify · Choose.

Centre
Regulate the system
Build physiological and emotional steadiness, so the person has greater capacity to respond.
Clarify
Understand what is happening
Make sense of symptoms, patterns, medical information, competing demands and what matters most.
Choose
Take the next workable step
Support agency, confidence, graded re-engagement and sustainable action.
Clinical scope

Where EF Health can help.

Gut–brain & persistent physical symptoms

Symptoms the body keeps producing

DGBIs, functional gastrointestinal symptoms, chronic nausea, symptom hypervigilance and health-related avoidance.

Adjustment to illness & medical care

The weight of being unwell

Diagnosis, treatment burden, procedures, recovery, uncertainty and changes in functioning or identity.

Pain, fatigue & nervous-system overload

When capacity runs low

Persistent pain, fatigue, sleep disruption, reduced capacity and difficulty re-engaging with daily life.

Health-related anxiety & functional disruption

When worry takes over

Health anxiety, panic, avoidance, fear of symptoms, reduced confidence and disruption to work, study or relationships.

EF Health does not replace medical investigation or treatment. Psychological care is integrated alongside the person's medical team.

The pathway

A clear, simple process.

1
Referral or enquiry
A clinician refers, or you make an enquiry.
2
Intake
You complete a structured intake.
3
Clinical review
We review suitability and fit.
4
Matched support
Matched to the right service or pathway.
5
Review & next steps
Progress reviewed and next steps agreed.

Completing an enquiry or intake does not constitute acceptance into clinical care. Clinical services begin after review and explicit acceptance.

Shared care

Psychological care that works alongside medicine.

EF Health collaborates with GPs, specialists and allied-health clinicians where appropriate and with consent. The aim is not to replace medical care, but to help patients understand symptoms, engage with treatment and rebuild function with greater confidence.

Consent-based communication

We share information with your treating team only with your consent.

Clear referral & review pathways

Structured referral, review and acceptance, so everyone knows the plan.

Practical updates for clinicians
