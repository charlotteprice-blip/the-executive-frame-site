EF|LAW × NZLS a national partnership.

Building practitioner capacity together — with the New Zealand Law Society, for early-career and family law practitioners across Aotearoa. One architecture. Two cohorts. Delivered through one trusted national channel.

The proposal The two cohorts
01 — The framework underneath

Before the proposal. The architecture EF|LAW sits inside.

Because EF|LAW is one application of a broader framework, it might help to introduce the underlying architecture before the cohort detail. The work is published at executiveframe.co.nz, and the same architecture sits underneath every arm of the practice — health, elite performance, organisational, family systems, neurodiversity, women in leadership, and law.

i.
The Executive Frame™
A structured psychological architecture
The Executive Frame™ is a structured psychological architecture for individuals, families, and organisations navigating pressure. It is clinician-developed, evidence-grounded, and built on a clinical observation: many performance breakdowns begin as regulatory ones. What can feel like overload is often a system carrying too much.
ii.
Eight arms. One framework.
Calibrated, not duplicated
EF|LAW is one of eight arms of the Executive Frame™ — same architecture, calibrated to the population that’s carrying it. The arms span clinical health, elite performance, organisational and corporate, legal practice, women in leadership, neurodiversity, family systems, and systems-level consulting. Each arm is a separate application of one architecture, not a separate method. That coherence is what allows the legal arm to be developed with the seriousness of a clinical programme rather than a bespoke wellbeing module.
iii.
The Frame doesn’t tell people to perform better
The order is biological. The work is structural.
The Frame maps what tends to narrow under pressure, and gives people architecture for each stage of that narrowing. The order is biological. The work is structural. Human first. Then performance.
02 — The C³ Loop™

Three moves held in sequence.

The C³ Loop™ is the operational mechanism inside the Frame. Three moves — Centre, Clarify, Choose — held in the order that matches how the regulatory system actually works. It is what participants take home and use the next morning.

01 · CENTRE
Stabilise
Emotional intelligence — the upstream layer
"Biology organises itself ahead of cognition."
Steady the nervous system before interpretation. Centre comes first because subcortical regulation is neurologically upstream of cortical reasoning. Skip it and the rest collapses.
02 · CLARIFY
Organise
Executive function — the middle layer
"Cognition before action."
Restore cognitive accuracy. Separate sensation from interpretation. Clarify only works once Centre is online — this is where executive function lives.
03 · CHOOSE
Act intentionally
Self-determination — the agency layer
"Toward what matters, not away from what frightens."
Translate clarity into action. One step, on purpose, in line with values. Choose becomes available once Centre and Clarify are in place.
→ REPEAT
Hold the sequence
The loop runs continuously
"The sequence holds the work."
Skip Centre and Clarify becomes anxious; skip Clarify and Choose becomes reactive. The loop is recursive — not a single intervention but a daily practice.
03 — The Tunnel™

The predictable degradation sequence.

Under sustained pressure, every system narrows in a predictable order. The Tunnel names that sequence so practitioners can recognise it early and exit it before it drives decisions.

01.
Instability
The regulatory system tilts out of range
Heart rate climbs. Sleep degrades. Sensation amplifies. The body can lose its steadiness. This is the earliest stage and the easiest one to intervene at — if the practitioner has the tools to recognise it.
02.
Confusion
Thinking narrows
Cognitive distortions rise. Interpretation hardens around threat. The mind can lose its accuracy. The practitioner becomes more certain, not less — and the certainty has narrowed onto a channel that does not match the full field.
03.
Powerlessness
Behaviour collapses inward
Avoidance, control, withdrawal. Agency narrows to immediate protection. Decision space contracts to short-term protective moves — which themselves compound the long-term load.
04 — S.H.I.F.T.™

The real-time tool. Under sixty seconds.

When the Tunnel activates, there are seconds before the cascade completes. S.H.I.F.T. is the under-sixty-second micro-intervention that interrupts it. It is not about being calm; it is about being strategic.

STEP 01 · S — STOP
Interrupt the reaction
Maps to Centre
"Notice the activation. Name it as it happens."
Pause the physical reaction. Name what’s happening. Linehan’s STOP skill from DBT.
STEP 02 · H — HOLD
Physiological Sigh
Maps to Centre
"Double inhale. Long exhale."
The fastest voluntary vagal activation in the evidence base. Bottom-up down-regulation before cognitive engagement.
STEP 03 · F — FOCUS
Fact, not story
Maps to Clarify
"Return to what is real."
Separate sensation from interpretation. One next action, within range — not the whole problem.
STEP 04 · T — TAKE ACTION
One step, on purpose
Maps to Choose
"Move deliberately. Aligned with how you want to operate."
The Deci–Ryan step. Autonomous action restored, with the agency the first three steps have rebuilt.

S.H.I.F.T. is not about being calm. It is about being strategic. You don’t eliminate the pressure. You operate inside it.

05 — The proposal

What we’re proposing.

EF|LAW is the legal arm of the Executive Frame™ — the same C³ Loop architecture, calibrated to the demand profile of legal practice. It sits deliberately alongside the NZLS wellbeing strategy rather than competing with it: where wellbeing programmes aim to reduce distress, EF|LAW aims to build the underlying capacity that makes high-quality practice sustainable across a career. The proposal is to bring two cohorts under that one shared architecture — the early-career stream and the family law stream — and offer them nationally through the NZLS as a single, coherent intervention.

Why this, why now

There are three reasons we think 2026 is the right window for a national-scale offer rather than a firm-by-firm one — and we’d genuinely value your read on whether this maps to what NZLS is hearing from members.

i.
The pace of practice is outrunning the tools most lawyers have
AI · always-on · transaction velocity
AI-augmented work, always-on communication, and accelerating transaction velocity are compressing the recovery windows that good professional judgement depends on. The biology hasn’t changed; the conditions have. Members deserve tools calibrated to the world they’re actually working in.
ii.
The early-career window is where this work is most leveraged
First seven years shape the career arc
The neuroscience of skill acquisition is fairly settled — the patterns built in the first seven years shape the whole arc of a career. Reaching practitioners early is materially kinder, and materially cheaper, than supporting recovery later.
iii.
Family law deserves a programme calibrated to it
A specialist, co-facilitated stream
Sustained adversarial pressure, acute client systems, and cumulative vicarious exposure produce a predictable performance-degradation pattern that generic offerings can’t address. A specialist, co-facilitated programme — clinician with senior family barrister — lands very differently in this group.

If any of this doesn’t match what you’re seeing from the membership, that’s exactly the conversation we’d like to have. The framework is calibrated to evidence, but the priority order should be calibrated to what NZLS knows about its members.

06 — The programme in detail

One operating system. Two calibrations.

Both cohorts share the same C³ Loop™ sequence and the same real-time tool, S.H.I.F.T.™. What changes between cohorts is calibration, depth of supervision, and delivery format — not the underlying framework. That coherence is what lets us offer NZLS one programme rather than two unrelated ones.

Cohort 01 — Early-Career Lawyers

0–7 PQE · National webinar series · 6×60-minute modules · 6.0 CPD hours · Clinician-led

Open to lawyers across the country regardless of firm size or practice area — designed so the resource is genuinely available to everyone the membership wants it to reach. Six standalone 60-minute modules, delivered live by webinar, building the complete C³ Loop. Every session is CPD-approved and lands with a real-time tool.

MODULE 01 · CENTRE
How You’re Wired to Thrive
Real-time tool: The Capacity Check
The baseline self-assessment. Identifies where your first binding constraint sits across Centre, Clarify, and Choose. The starting point for the rest of the programme.
MODULE 02 · CENTRE
The Biology of Performing Well
Real-time tool: The Physiology Reset
The neurosequential principle. Why regulation must come first. The Physiological Sigh and the under-sixty-second reset deployable between meetings, before court, after the hard call.
MODULE 03 · CENTRE
Connection Is Neurologically Necessary
Real-time tool: The Emotional Airlock
Goleman’s EI framework applied to legal practice. Naming what transmits between practitioner and client — and the structured technique for preventing contamination across cases.
MODULE 04 · CENTRE → CLARIFY
From Reaction to Response
Real-time tool: The Pause Protocol
The bridge module. The full S.H.I.F.T.™ protocol taught in detail. The exit from the Tunnel, the move from reactive to deliberate — consistent with Linehan’s DBT distress tolerance work.
MODULE 05 · CLARIFY
Stress Is Information
Real-time tool: The Reframe Check
McGonigal’s stress-mindset science. Reframing activation as mobilisation, not threat. Beck-informed cognitive distortion recognition in real-time decision-making under load.
MODULE 06 · CHOOSE
Your Sustainable Performance System
Real-time tool: The Performance Map
Deci & Ryan’s self-determination theory applied to legal practice. The architecture of a sustainable career. The Performance Map ties the whole programme together.

Cohort 02 — Family Law (Specialist Stream)

0–7 PQE and 8+ PQE streams · Three half-day modules across 16 weeks · In-person + supervision · 15 CPD hours · Co-facilitated

A specialist programme co-facilitated by Dr Anticich and a senior family barrister. Three half-day in-person modules across a 16-week arc, with small-group online supervision between modules to help the work transfer into live practice. Streamed by seniority so each cohort can speak openly without the dynamics of mixed seniority getting in the way of regulatory work.

MODULE 01 · CENTRE · WEEK 0
Regulation Foundation
Perry · Linehan · Goleman
What gets installed: S.H.I.F.T.™, window-of-tolerance framework, pre-engagement worksheet, the Tunnel™ clinical model. Standalone value for practitioners with regulatory foundations to build.
MODULE 02 · CLARIFY · WEEK 8
Cognition Under Load
Barkley · Beck
What gets installed: the C³ Loop™, Beck-informed cognitive bias field guide calibrated to family law, ethical clarity tools for live values conflicts.
MODULE 03 · CHOOSE · WEEK 16
Agency & Sustainability
Deci & Ryan · Dweck · McGonigal
What gets installed: boundary architecture, motivational design, growth-orientation reframe of fixed professional identities. Long-horizon sustainability work.

Two 90-minute small-group online supervision sessions sit between the modules (weeks 4 and 12) — the mechanism that converts teaching into embedded practice. Optional 90-day post-programme integration supervision extends the arc for practitioners who elect it.

07 — What each module includes

The asset set. Login-ready bundles.

All previously circulated EF|LAW content will be re-authored under the consolidated 2026 architecture so each module ships as a complete, login-ready bundle. Our intention is that whatever a member opens in the portal, it reads, looks, and behaves as part of one programme — same voice, same visual system, same user experience throughout.

i.
eLearning module
Self-paced · ~20–30 minutes · voiceover-ready · mobile-friendly
The core teaching for the module, delivered as self-paced eLearning. Designed to be completed in one sitting or returned to in fragments. Voiceover-ready for accessibility; mobile-friendly for between-appointment use.
ii.
Participant workbook
Fillable PDF · reflection prompts · CPD integration template
Fillable PDF with structured reflection prompts, exercises, and a CPD-aligned integration template. Members can complete it inside the portal or download to keep.
iii.
Real-time tool / desk card
Capacity Check · S.H.I.F.T.™ · Now–Do–Later · Emotional Airlock · Agency Map · Performance Map
The portable tool installed by that module. One card per module, designed to be deployed in live practice. The infrastructure under load — designed on the clinical principle that under pressure, practitioners reach for what is physically accessible.
iv.
Live session
Webinar (early-career) or co-facilitated half-day (family law)
The live element — webinar for the early-career stream, in-person half-day seminar for the family law stream. Recorded and available afterwards inside the portal.
v.
CPDPR documentation pack
Pre-mapped to NZLS competency areas
Pre-written learning objectives mapped to NZLS competency areas. A compliant CPD entry in minutes, not hours. Less admin friction for members, less verification work for NZLS.
08 — Hosting on the NZLS member portal

One login. One place. One experience.

Our preference — if it works for NZLS — is for the member portal to be the canonical home for all of this. One login, one place, one experience that feels properly part of NZLS membership rather than bolted to the side of it. We’d love your team’s view on what’s feasible inside the current portal architecture; the proposal below is a starting point we can adjust together.

i.
Single sign-on for NZLS members
No friction at the asset level
Members access the programme through their existing NZLS login. No separate account creation, no password proliferation.
ii.
Dedicated module pages
All assets in one place
Each module hosted as a dedicated page — live-session recording, eLearning, workbook (PDF and fillable), tool / desk card, and CPDPR template all in one place.
iii.
Progress tracking & certificates
Light-touch, member-facing
Light progress tracking and a printable certificate of completion per module. Members can see where they are in the arc.
iv.
LawTalk & member-portal placements
Built with your communications team
LawTalk feature schedule and member-portal placements aligned to each module release. Built with NZLS communications, not bolted on.
v.
Aggregate engagement data
De-identified · quarterly
De-identified aggregate engagement data shared with NZLS quarterly so we can both see what’s working and refine over time.

10 — Why we think this works for NZLS

Clean to communicate. Easy to govern.

i.
Clean to communicate, easy to govern
One programme, two cohorts, one channel
Simple to introduce, simple to renew. The two cohorts share one operating system, which means one message to members and one set of governance arrangements with NZLS.
ii.
Reinforces NZLS as the trusted home of practitioner capacity
Part of membership, not an external link
Hosting inside the portal makes this part of membership, not an external link members have to chase. Members find capacity-building infrastructure in the same place they find everything else NZLS provides.
iii.
CPD-compliant by design
Pre-mapped CPDPR template per asset
Every asset ships with a pre-mapped CPDPR template — less admin friction for members, less verification work for NZLS.
iv.
Clinically authored, professionally specific
Co-facilitated where the demand profile requires it
Co-facilitated where the demand profile requires it (family law); clinician-led where scale matters (early-career). Right format for each context.

Capacity infrastructure, not wellbeing comms
A complement to the existing wellbeing work
