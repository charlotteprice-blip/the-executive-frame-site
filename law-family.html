EF|LAW — FAMILY
A performance architecture for family law practitioners.
Not wellbeing. Not resilience training.
The systematic installation of regulatory, metacognitive, and motivational infrastructure for adversarial practice.
Family law is a performance environment. Practitioners work under sustained affective load, in adversarial systems designed to escalate, holding competing ethical obligations, with client systems in acute collapse. Doctrine and procedure do not address this. Wellbeing programmes do not address this. What is required is a performance architecture. EF|Law — Family is that architecture — three half-day modules across a 16-week arc, co-facilitated by a clinical psychologist and a senior family barrister, with between-module supervision and a full practitioner tool suite. 15 CPD hours. NZLS CPDPR-aligned.

The Family Law programme is being proposed as Cohort 02 of an NZLS national partnership for 2026 — alongside an early-career webinar series for the 0–7 PQE stream. Read the partnership proposal →

The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Stabilise the nervous system first. Subcortical regulatory systems must be on-line before cortical function can run cleanly — this is Perry's neurosequential principle. The S.H.I.F.T. Protocol™ and DBT distress tolerance (Linehan) give practitioners deployable tools for the courtroom corridor, the mediation, the disclosure that lands hard. Goleman's EI gives the vocabulary for what transmits between practitioner and client.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Metacognition protected from reactivity. Barkley's executive function research establishes cognition-under-load as a trainable capacity. Beck's cognitive model identifies the distortions active in family law: catastrophising, confirmation bias, rescue fantasy, ethical drift. The C³ Loop™ is structured executive function activation — the steady-state tool that protects judgment when the field is contested.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Values-aligned action across a career. Deci & Ryan's self-determination theory identifies autonomy, competence, and relatedness as the substrate of sustainable practice. Dweck's growth-orientation work re-frames fixed professional identities as trainable capacities. McGonigal's stress-mindset science turns adversarial load from threat into mobilisation. The architecture of a long-horizon family law career.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is architectural, not conceptual. Centre must precede Clarify because subcortical activation cuts cortical reasoning off from its regulatory substrate — you cannot reason your way out of activation. Clarify must precede Choose because values-aligned action cannot land on top of compressed metacognition. Skip the order and you reliably produce ethical drift, cognitive distortion, and the career-level attrition the profession experiences at clinically significant rates.

C³ Law Series · National Professional Development Programme 2026
Six modules. The Executive Frame™ from day one.
Three half-day in-person modules across a 16-week arc, co-facilitated by Dr Sarah Anticich and a senior family barrister. Each module is a complete standalone offering AND a sequential component of the full programme. Small-group online supervision sits between each module (weeks 4 and 12), extending the learning into live practice during the eight-week gaps. Two parallel cohorts: 0–7 years PQE and 8+ years PQE, with content calibrated to each demand profile.

MODULE 01 · CENTRE
The Regulation Foundation
Perry · Goleman · Linehan
"You cannot reason your way out of activation."
Week 0. Opens the programme. Establishes the performance architecture and teaches the S.H.I.F.T. Protocol™. Participants map their window of tolerance, identify emotional patterns via Goleman's EI model, and leave with a personal regulatory anchor and pocket tools. The Tunnel™ is introduced as the clinical model they are learning to recognise and exit. Standalone value: SHIFT protocol, DBT distress tolerance skills, window-of-tolerance framework, pre-engagement worksheet. Even without Modules 2 and 3, participants leave with tools they can deploy the next morning.
MODULE 02 · CLARIFY
Cognition Under Load
Barkley · Beck
"Under pressure, executive function does not disappear. It simplifies."
Week 8. Builds metacognitive capacity — observing one's own reasoning while it is happening, noticing where it is being driven by reactivity, assumption, or bias rather than evidence. The C³ Loop™ introduced as the steady-state operational tool. Beck's cognitive model applied to family law's most active distortions: confirmation bias, catastrophising, rescue fantasy, moral disengagement. Standalone value: C³ Loop, Beck-informed cognitive bias framework calibrated to family law, ethical clarity tools for live values conflicts.
MODULE 03 · CHOOSE
Agency and Sustainable Practice
Deci & Ryan · Dweck · McGonigal
"Sustainability is architectural, not aspirational."
Week 16. Moves from in-the-moment regulation to career-horizon sustainability. Examines boundary design, client selection, motivation, and identity integration through Self-Determination Theory, growth-mindset research (Dweck), and stress-mindset science (McGonigal). Closes the programme and hands the framework over to the practitioner to own. Standalone value: boundary architecture design, motivational audit, growth-orientation reframe of fixed professional identities, long-horizon sustainability framework. Works as a standalone offering for senior practitioners examining career sustainability.
MODULE S1 · SUPERVISION
Integration Supervision · Week 4
Between Module 01 and Module 02
"Supervision closes the gap where attrition happens."
One 90-minute small-group online session, 4–6 practitioners. Live application of the EF framework to a current file — for regulatory, cognitive, and ethical review rather than legal advice. Facilitated by the clinical lead, the legal lead, or jointly. Confidential. Chatham House rule; case material de-identified. The mechanism that converts teaching into embedded practice.
MODULE S2 · SUPERVISION
Integration Supervision · Week 12
Between Module 02 and Module 03
"From teaching to embedded practice."
Second 90-minute supervision session, same format. Case presentation by one practitioner (25 min), facilitated group reflection (35 min), individual behavioural commitments for the fortnight ahead (20 min). Each session opens with a brief structured regulatory check-in and closes with one named behavioural commitment per practitioner.

Law Toolkit
Eight tools. Immediately usable.
Each usable without prior training. Designed for the real-time moments of your work.

A5 tear-off pad, 50 sheets. Structured around the highest-leverage sections of the framework. Completed in 10–15 minutes before client meetings.
Pre-engagement Worksheet Pad
Companion to the pre-meeting worksheet. Used at intake to map file profile, client capacity, boundary architecture, and scope.
C³ Loop™ Field Card
Pocket-sized laminated reference. Designed for the courtroom corridor, between-appointments use, and mid-negotiation deployment.

Companion card for acute activation moments. The four-step sequence in the format practitioners actually reach for under load.
