Overview

What the work builds.

Teacher training builds pedagogy. EF|Education builds the human capacity that sustains it — because classrooms run on regulated adults, and the conditions of the job place real, measurable pressure on the nervous system.

Regulate

Recognise pressure states early — in yourself and in the room — and restore composure before they set the tone.

Think clearly

Protect planning, judgement, and attention when workload, behaviour, and urgency compete.

Act deliberately

Build sustainable ways of working within the real constraints of a school year — chosen, not defaulted to.

How it's delivered

Built for schools, not added to them.

For educators
Workshops and PLD sessions on regulation, executive function, and sustainable practice — practical tools usable the next morning.
For school leaders
Leadership development and advisory — the conditions for sound judgement and a staffroom that recovers.
For learning communities
A shared, psychologically accurate language for pressure — across staff, students, and whānau.
Grounding
Led by Dr Sarah Anticich, Clinical and Industrial/Organisational Psychologist — doctoral research on the prevention of anxiety and the development of emotional resilience in children and adolescents.
