The model

Human first.
Then performance.

EF Health applies the Executive Frame™ model to people living with disorders of gut-brain interaction. It addresses the psychological layer that sits alongside biomedical care — the regulatory, cognitive, and motivational barriers that limit treatment engagement and quality of life.

Developed by Dr Sarah Anticich, Clinical and Industrial Organisational Psychologist, EF Health is a structured, evidence-based delivery system built specifically for the gut-brain experience.

The program operates through the C³ Loop — Centre, Clarify, Choose — grounded in Emotional Intelligence, Executive Functioning, and Self-Determination Theory.

Stability — Emotional Intelligence
Regulation, recovery, and reducing the physiological stress that amplifies gut symptoms.
Clarity — Executive Functioning
Organising thinking, managing complexity, and reducing the cognitive burden of chronic illness.
Agency — Self-Determination
Restoring ownership, rebuilding confidence, and reconnecting action to values.
Context Load
Understanding the environmental and systems pressure that shapes what is actually sustainable.
Who this is for

Built for gut-brain conditions

Adults, adolescents, and families referred through MacMurray Clinic by their GP or gastroenterologist.

IBS

Irritable Bowel Syndrome — abdominal pain, altered bowel habit, symptom fluctuation with stress

Functional Dyspepsia

Persistent upper GI symptoms without structural cause, often linked to visceral hypersensitivity

Functional Constipation / Diarrhoea

Disrupted motility without organic explanation, frequently co-occurring with anxiety and avoidance

SIBO / Motility Disorders

Complex symptom profiles requiring multi-system management and sustained self-regulation support

Crohn's Disease

Significant psychological burden, flare-remission cycles, identity disruption, and adherence difficulty

Other Chronic Illness

Generalisable to any condition where gut-brain dysregulation is a clinical concern

The process

How EF Health works

From referral to program completion.

1
Referral
GP or gastroenterologist submits Smart Referral Form. Urgency level determines contact timeframe.
2
Intake
Patient completes gut-brain intake form. Scored profile generated across four domains.
3
Program
Clinician allocates pathway — psychoeducation, group, eLearning, or individual — matched to profile.
4
Review
Progress reviewed at mid-program and completion. Profile reassessed. Next steps determined.
For referring clinicians

Built for clinical integration

EF Health complements, not replaces, biomedical and gastroenterological care. Every referral is structured, tracked, and reported back to the treating team.

The Smart Referral Form captures the clinical picture in full and routes directly into the MacMurray Clinic DGBI program. Shared care, progress updates, and discharge summaries are available for all referring clinicians.
