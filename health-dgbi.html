Intervention pathways

One stepped-care system. Five responsive pathways.

Everyone begins with EF Health Foundations, then continues on the pathway confirmed for them — all on the same Executive Frame™ sequence: Centre, Clarify, Choose. Together the pathways form one stepped-care system; final pathway allocation follows review and clinical acceptance.

The entry point for most participants — delivered as Gut Sense, a half-day gut–brain foundations workshop, or a self-paced orientation. Make sense of why symptoms persist and get your footing back.

The EF Health programme

Foundations, then four sessions.

EF Health begins with Foundations — Gut Sense, a half-day gut–brain workshop (or a self-paced orientation) — followed, where appropriate, by the four-session EF Health Group Programme. Core psychoeducation is delivered in Foundations and reinforced throughout. Exact scheduling is confirmed with you.

Foundations · Gut Sense
Understand & settle

What a DGBI is, why symptoms persist, and first tools to steady the nervous system — the half-day workshop or a self-paced orientation.

Session 1 · Centre
Regulate the system

Settle the nervous system that drives symptoms — building a reliable, personal calming practice.

Session 2 · Clarify
Separate symptom & story

The gut–brain axis explained without alarm; working with hypervigilance and flares.

Session 3 · Choose
Food, values & pacing

Easing food-related anxiety and choosing values-based action at a sustainable pace.

Session 4 · Re-engage & Sustain
Live alongside it

Re-engaging with what matters, relapse-prevention, and a personal plan for the long term.

For referrers

Confident, collaborative referrals.

EF Health is designed to slot into existing DGBI care — clear about who benefits, quick to respond, and communicative throughout.

Who should refer

Patients with IBS, functional dyspepsia, chronic nausea, functional constipation or motility disorders — especially where symptom hypervigilance, health anxiety or food-related anxiety are prominent.

How referrals work

GP, gastroenterologist, surgeon or allied health refer via the secure Smart Referral form (about 10–12 minutes); patients then complete an online intake (about 10 minutes) before clinical review and triage. Self-enquiries are welcomed and guided to the right next step.

Response time

Referrals are acknowledged promptly. We will contact the patient and referrer once the referral and intake have been reviewed.

Reports & updates

With consent, referrers receive brief, practical updates — formulation, pathway and progress — written to be useful in a busy clinic.

Shared care

Psychological care runs alongside medical management, never instead of it — coordinated so the whole team is working to one plan.

Clinician resources

Downloadable briefings and patient-facing sheets you can print or share at the point of referral, so patients arrive informed.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

Can I refer myself?

EF Health is a referred service, delivered in partnership with the MacMurray Centre. For clinical care we ask your GP or gastroenterologist to refer you — and we're happy to guide you on how to ask. Self-initiated contact is managed as an enquiry; completing an enquiry or intake does not by itself confirm acceptance into clinical care — entry into clinical pathways follows review and clinical acceptance by EF Health. You can also access our self-paced educational resources at any time, or take the Capacity Check to get oriented.

What happens after I start an intake?

Your intake is reviewed by a psychologist, who considers your history and goals and confirms the right pathway — EF Health Foundations, the Group Programme, the Self-Paced Programme, or an Individual Pathway. You'll know your next step quickly.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the pathway and are confirmed at intake, along with any funding or insurance options that may apply.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Sessions are available in-person and by secure telehealth across New Zealand, so you can take part from wherever you are — including if you live outside the main centres.

Is what I share confidential?

Your sessions are confidential. With your consent, we share brief, practical updates with your referrer or medical team so your care stays coordinated. We'll always be clear about what is shared and why.

How is this different from seeing any psychologist?

EF Health is built specifically for disorders of gut–brain interaction. It combines gut–brain science with the Executive Frame™ and evidence-based therapies (CBT, ACT, CFT, gut-directed hypnotherapy), and it's delivered as part of integrated medical care.
