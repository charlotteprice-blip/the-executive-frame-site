The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
The gut-brain axis is a regulatory circuit. Symptom flares often follow autonomic activation by hours, not minutes — making the link invisible without the framework. Settling the nervous system is not adjunct therapy. It is the substrate the gut needs to function clearly.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Separate symptom from interpretation, sensation from threat. Working memory and cognitive flexibility tools that work alongside medical treatment — not in place of it. Naming what the body is doing without escalating it.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Operate on your own terms within a body that has its own signals. Autonomy in food choices, social planning, work pace. Competence grounded in your own evidence about what your system does. A life chosen, not negotiated around symptoms.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because the dysregulated gut-brain system amplifies threat signals from inside the body itself. Clarify must precede Choose because life cannot be designed from symptom-driven reactivity. This is biology — applied to the specific architecture of disorders of gut-brain interaction.
