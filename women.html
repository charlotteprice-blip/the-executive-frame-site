The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Emotional regulation before negotiation, decision, or response. The body cannot be hacked into compliance — it has to be regulated. Settle the system before the room asks for performance. The physiological foundation under sustained identity load.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Separate the load that is yours from the load that has been delegated to you without your consent. Working memory and cognitive flexibility protected from the cost of constant calibration. Make the invisible visible.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Lead from values, not from constant recalibration to whichever room you're in. Decide which loads are yours to carry. The rest gets returned. Autonomy, competence, and connection on your terms.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because identity load registers in the body before it registers in language. Clarify must precede Choose because the invisible must be named before it can be redesigned. This is biology — and it applies the same way regardless of who is carrying the load.
