What · The Framework

The architecture under the work.

Three moves held in sequence. Centre. Clarify. Choose. Each step is neurologically upstream of the next — skip Centre and Clarify becomes anxious; skip Clarify and Choose becomes reactive. The order matches how the regulatory system actually works. The sequence holds the work.

The science The method (S.H.I.F.T.™)
The Thesis · What the framework addresses
You are not failing. You are overloaded.
Many performance breakdowns begin as regulatory ones. What can feel like overload is often a system carrying too much. The Executive Frame™ does not tell people to perform better — it maps what tends to narrow under pressure, and gives them architecture for each stage.
Stage 01 · EI
Centre
Stabilise
Bring the regulatory system back inside its working range. Heart rate, sleep, sensation — the body settles before the mind can read accurately. Perry's neurosequential principle, Goleman's EI, Linehan's distress tolerance.
Stage 02 · EF
Clarify
Organise
Separate fact from interpretation. Restore working memory, inhibition, and cognitive flexibility. Barkley's executive function research and Beck's cognitive model. Thinking that matches the actual situation.
Stage 03 · SDT
Choose
Act intentionally
Move deliberately. One step, on purpose, aligned with how you want to operate. Deci & Ryan's self-determination theory, Dweck's growth orientation, McGonigal's stress-mindset science.
The C³ Loop™
Centre. Clarify. Choose. Repeat.
C¹CENTRE — stabilise the regulatory system
C²CLARIFY — organise thinking, separate fact from story
C³CHOOSE — act intentionally, one step on purpose
↺REPEAT — return to Centre. The loop is recursive.

Skip Centre and Clarify becomes anxious. Skip Clarify and Choose becomes reactive. The sequence holds the work — and that is why the Executive Frame™ is sequential, not optional, not à la carte. This is biology, not preference.

The Tunnel · The predictable degradation sequence
What the architecture is built against.
Under sustained pressure, every system narrows in a predictable order. Naming it is the first move. The Tunnel is not a wellbeing failure — it is the shape pressure takes when the architecture is not yet installed.
STAGE 01 · CENTRE COMPROMISED
Instability
The body loses its steadiness
"Heart rate climbs. Sleep degrades. Sensation amplifies."
The regulatory system tilts out of range. Subcortical activation pre-loaded by prior exposure. The body can lose its steadiness — and cortical engagement degrades before the first word of the meeting, conversation, or matter.
STAGE 02 · CLARIFY COMPROMISED
Confusion
The mind loses its accuracy
"Cognitive distortions rise. Interpretation hardens."
Thinking narrows. Interpretation hardens around threat. The mind can lose its accuracy. The practitioner becomes more certain, not less — and the certainty has narrowed onto a channel that does not match the full field.
STAGE 03 · CHOOSE COMPROMISED
Constriction
Agency narrows to protection
"Behaviour collapses inward."
Avoidance, control, withdrawal. Decision space contracts. Agency can narrow to immediate protection — and the pattern, repeated, produces the career-level attrition the professions experience at clinically significant rates.
