The organisational challenge

As pressure rises, systems narrow.

As pressure rises, attention narrows, urgency increases, and coordination becomes harder. These are predictable human responses — but in an organisation, they can quickly become patterns of communication, decision-making, and culture.

The aim is not to remove pressure. It is to help the system recognise narrowing early, restore sufficient steadiness, and make deliberate choices before urgency becomes the operating model.

The C³ approach

Centre · Clarify · Choose,
at the level of the system.

01

Centre

Emotional intelligence · EI

Establish the conditions for clear thinking — steadiness, psychological safety, and a shared read of the pressure in the system.

02

Clarify

Executive function · EF

Establish a reliable view of the work — what is happening, what matters most, and where responsibility and decisions sit.

03

Choose

Self-determination theory · SDT

Establish deliberate action under constraint — coordinated choices that reflect purpose, standards, and real capacity.

In practice, the sequence is carried by a small set of working tools — the C³ Loop™, The Tunnel™, and the S.H.I.F.T.™ reset — so leaders and teams share one language for recognising narrowing and responding deliberately.

Evidence & methodology

Established science, applied in sequence.

The framework draws on established work in emotional regulation, executive functioning, and self-determination theory. These bodies of evidence are applied through a practical sequence: steady the system, clarify what matters, and choose deliberately. The science page sets out the fuller basis.

Informing literatures include the work of Perry, Goleman, and Linehan; Barkley, Beck, and Ericsson; and Deci & Ryan.
