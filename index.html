The thesis — what the framework addresses
Many performance breakdowns begin as regulatory ones

What can feel like overload
is often a system carrying too much.

The Executive Frame™ does not tell people to perform better. It maps what tends to narrow under pressure, and gives them architecture for each stage of that narrowing. The order is biological. The work is structural. Stop coping. Start designing.

The C³ Loop™ — three moves held in sequence
The operational mechanism

Three moves
held in sequence.

Centre. Clarify. Choose. The order matches how the regulatory system actually works.

Stage 01 · EI Centre T

Stabilise the nervous system before interpretation. Biology organises itself ahead of cognition.

Stage 02 · EF Clarify T

Restore cognitive accuracy. Separate sensation from interpretation. Cognition before action.

Stage 03 · SDT Choose T

Translate clarity into action. Toward what matters, not away from what frightens.

Skip Centre and Clarify becomes anxious. Skip Clarify and Choose becomes reactive. The sequence holds the work.

The Tunnel — the predictable degradation sequence
What the architecture is built against

The Tunnel —
the predictable degradation sequence.

Under sustained pressure, every system narrows in a predictable order. Naming it is the first move.

Instability
→
The regulatory system tilts out of range. Heart rate climbs. Sleep degrades. Sensation amplifies. The body can lose its steadiness.
Confusion
→
Thinking narrows. Cognitive distortions rise. Interpretation hardens around threat. The mind can lose its accuracy.
Powerlessness
→
Behaviour collapses inward. Avoidance, control, withdrawal. Agency narrows to immediate protection. Agency can narrow.
Run in sequence · Under sixty seconds

S.H.I.F.T.™

When The Tunnel activates, you have seconds before the cascade completes. S.H.I.F.T. interrupts it — not about being calm, about being strategic.

S
