The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
The parental nervous system has to settle before the family system can. Children regulate through their parents — the body is the first signal. Stabilise the parent before working on the child's behaviour. The physiological foundation of family co-regulation.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Separate behaviour from interpretation. What is yours, what is your child's, what is the system's. Reduce the cognitive load on the family. Cognitive tools that work in the household, not just on the page.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Choose responses that grow the system, not extract from it. Lead the family from architecture, not reactivity. Build capacity inside the home. The architecture of a family system that compounds rather than erodes.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because dysregulated parents cannot read their children clearly. Clarify must precede Choose because behaviour-driven decisions are not architecture. This is biology — and the family system runs on the same biology as any other human work.
