01 — The pathways

Where the work happens. And how to begin.

The first conversation is always the same: a brief enquiry, a short call, and a recommendation tailored to your context. From there, the work unfolds through one of three pathways.

Pathway 01 · Individual clinical
By referral or direct enquiry

Individual psychology, held with depth.

For individuals navigating sustained clinical, professional, or relational pressure. Sessions held in Auckland, Christchurch, Queenstown, or by telehealth. Specialist focus on the populations Sarah has worked with longest — senior counsel, executives, clinicians, women carrying complex load, and patients with chronic functional conditions.

Typical engagements: 6–20 sessions over 3–9 months. Some long-arc work continues quarterly.

Pathway 02 · Organisational engagement
Boards · Executive teams · Practices

Performance without burning the people.

For organisations whose performance challenges are structural rather than individual — legal practices, medical specialist centres, professional service firms, executive teams. Engagements include diagnostic assessment, leadership coaching, programme design, team-level work, and ongoing organisational consultation. Delivered as engagement, not content.

Typical engagements: 3-month diagnostic, 6–12-month programme, or ongoing partnership.

Clinical · Legal · Real estate

Embedded with the organisations we partner with.

02 — What to expect

The first conversation. And what follows.

01

Brief enquiry

Submit the enquiry form. A few details is enough. Don’t worry about getting it “right” — the form is structured to help us understand context.

02

Triage & reply

Read carefully within one business day, often sooner. Sarah or her practice team responds with a recommendation matched to what you’ve shared.

03

Initial conversation

A focused first session or discovery call. Clinical assessment for individual work; scoping conversation for organisational engagements.

04

Engagement begins

If the fit is right, the work begins. Clear scope, clear cadence, clear endpoint. No long contracts. Re-evaluation at agreed milestones.
