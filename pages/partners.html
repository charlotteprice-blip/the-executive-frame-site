Become a partner
For organisations interested in formal partnership.
Executive Frame™ partnerships take three primary forms — clinical referral pathways, CPD provider relationships, and in-house staff portal delivery. Each begins with a confidential scoping conversation.
CLINICAL PARTNERSHIP
Referral pathway integration
For specialist clinics and medical practices
Integrated clinical pathways where patients receive medical care and Executive Frame™ psychological support as a co-developed programme. McMurray Gastroenterology is the current model.
CPD & PROFESSIONAL BODIES
National CPD provider
For professional regulatory bodies
CPD-accredited programme delivery to a profession at scale. Module accreditation, certificate of completion, member-portal access. NZLS is the current model.
IN-HOUSE STAFF PORTAL
Firm or organisation portal
For firms, agencies, and organisations
A confidential internal portal providing the Executive Frame™ tool suite, supervision pathways, and coaching access to staff. Wynn Williams and Harcourts Gold are current models.
