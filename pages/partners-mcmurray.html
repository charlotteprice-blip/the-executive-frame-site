The Partnership

Two specialties. One clinical conversation.

McMurray Endoscopy & Specialist Centre

A specialist gastroenterology centre providing diagnostic endoscopy, colonoscopy, and clinical management for patients across the upper and lower gastrointestinal tract. McMurray sees a high volume of patients whose symptoms fall under the framework of disorders of gut–brain interaction — functional dyspepsia, IBS, abdominal pain, reflux, bloating, altered motility.

For many of these patients, structural investigation returns normal results — and the symptoms remain. This is where the clinical conversation often needs to widen.

Specialist gastroenterology · Auckland

Executive Frame™ Health

The clinical health arm of Executive Frame™, led by Dr Sarah Anticich — clinical and industrial/organisational psychologist. EF Health works specifically with the psychological architecture of chronic and functional symptoms: gut-focused anxiety, visceral hypervigilance, autonomic dysregulation, and the cognitive-behavioural patterns that maintain symptoms over time.

The work integrates evidence-based gut-directed psychological intervention — gut-directed hypnotherapy, exposure-based protocols, autonomic regulation, and the C³ Loop™ framework for sustained symptom management.
