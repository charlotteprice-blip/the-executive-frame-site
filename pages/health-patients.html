The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Stabilise the body first. Visceral hypersensitivity and central sensitisation are real, neurobiological, and trainable. The first work is regulating the system that is amplifying the sensation — not overriding the sensation itself. This is where lasting change starts.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Separate sensation from story. Under symptom load, thinking distorts in predictable ways — catastrophising, health anxiety, hypervigilance. Naming the patterns is the second move. The gap between the fact (the sensation) and the story (the interpretation) is where the work lives.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Act in line with what matters. The ultimate impairment of DGBI is not the symptom — it is the progressive narrowing of life around avoiding symptoms. Values-aligned action, paced, with the agency the first two steps restored. A life that is bigger than the illness.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The order is biology, not preference. You cannot think clearly from inside symptom activation. You cannot make values-aligned choices on top of catastrophic interpretation. Centre. Clarify. Choose. — in that order, always. This is how the system actually works, and it is why this approach holds when other approaches have not.

Body before brain. Always.

For DGBI nervous systems, dysregulation comes faster, hits harder, and clears slower. Centre is the precondition for every executive function downstream. Skip it and the rest collapses.

Module 01
Module 01 · Foundations

Your gut is talking. Your brain is listening.

The gut–brain axis as the foundation for everything that follows. You are not imagining your symptoms. They are real. And they are neurobiologically driven.

Bidirectional axis

The enteric nervous system

Vagus nerve, immune signalling, microbiome.
The gut's own network of neurons communicates bidirectionally with the brain through the vagus nerve, immune signalling, and the microbiome. When the threat system is activated, gut function changes. When the gut is in distress, the brain reads threat. This is not metaphor — it's measurable neurobiology.
Threat system

Detection & protection

Cortisol. Vigilance. Symptom amplification.
When Threat dominates — as it often does in chronic illness — gut symptoms amplify. The nervous system reads sensation as danger and recruits more attention to it. The symptom and the system carrying it become a feedback loop.
Drive system

Pursuit & achievement

Dopamine. Push-through. Recovery debt.
Drive can mask DGBI symptoms — until it can't. The pattern of pushing through a flare to meet a deadline borrows from regulatory reserves that are already depleted. The cost is a longer, deeper symptom episode afterwards.
Soothe system

Safety, care, contentment

Oxytocin. Parasympathetic. Recovery.
When Soothe is accessible, gut function improves. Heart rate slows, muscles relax, the gut's regulatory environment normalises. The system most often missing in DGBI cognition — and the system that can be retrained.

Module 02
Module 02 · The Tunnel

The Tunnel. What happens when symptoms spike.

The three-stage predictable degradation pattern, mapped to the gut–brain experience. Sensation rises, thinking narrows, behaviour contracts. Naming the Tunnel is the first move toward exiting it.

Stage 01 · Instability

Sensation rises

The body loses its steadiness first.
Visceral sensitivity climbs. Sleep degrades. The regulatory range narrows. You can feel the system tilting out of range before you can name what's happening — and naming it is the Centre work.
Stage 02 · Confusion

Thinking narrows

The mind loses accuracy.
Catastrophic interpretation hardens. Health anxiety amplifies. You become more certain, not less — and the certainty has narrowed onto a channel that does not match the full field of what's actually happening.
Stage 03 · Constriction

Behaviour contracts

Agency narrows to protection.
Plans cancelled. Foods restricted. Activities avoided. Decision space contracts. Only short-term protective moves remain available — and the protective moves themselves compound the long-term load.
The pattern

Predictable, not personal

Same architecture every time.
The Tunnel is not character. It's the predictable shape pressure takes when the architecture isn't yet installed. Mapping yours — your specific signals, in order — is what makes intervention possible early.

Module 03
Module 03 · S.H.I.F.T.™

S.H.I.F.T.™ Your real-time reset.

The four-step regulation sequence, adapted for DGBI. Run in sequence. Under sixty seconds. At symptom onset — not to suppress the symptom, but to prevent the secondary anxiety and threat activation that amplifies it.

S · Stop

Interrupt the reaction

Notice both emotional activation and gut sensation.
Pause the physical reaction. Name what's happening as it happens. The deliberate interruption that precedes all other regulatory work — consistent with Linehan's STOP skill in DBT.
H · Hold (physiological sigh)

Vagal down-regulation

Double inhale through the nose. Long exhale through the mouth.
The fastest voluntary vagal activation in the evidence base. Down-regulates the autonomic system before any cognitive engagement. Three repetitions. The single step most often skipped.
F · Focus

Fact, not story

One next action that is not symptom-focused.
Return to what is real. Separate the sensation (fact) from the catastrophic interpretation (story). Identify the next action — small, deliberate, not aimed at the symptom itself.
T · Take action

Values-aligned next step

One move, on purpose.
Move deliberately, in line with how you want to operate. Not the whole solution — one step, named and committed to, with the agency the first three steps have restored.

Module 04
Module 04 · Story vs Fact

The story your gut tells. Cognitive patterns in DGBI.

Under symptom load, thinking distorts in predictable ways. The cognitive layer that compounds the experience of symptoms beyond their biological source. Three patterns appear repeatedly.

Pattern 01

Symptom catastrophising

This will never end. It's only getting worse.
The mind extrapolates the current moment forward in time and concludes the worst. Catastrophising compresses the future onto a single trajectory — and that trajectory is amplification, not recovery.
Pattern 02

Health anxiety amplification

This must mean something is seriously wrong.
Sensation gets read as evidence of underlying disease. Hyper-vigilance increases. Every symptom becomes a signal — and the signal-detection itself feeds the threat system that is amplifying the symptom.
Pattern 03

Hypervigilance

Interoceptive scanning, all day.
The body is watched so closely that sensation gets amplified by attention alone. What you attend to grows. Hypervigilance is exhausting and counter-productive — and it can be retrained.
The intervention

Fact vs Story

Sensation is fact. Meaning is story.
Both are real. They are not the same thing. The gap between the fact (the sensation) and the story (the interpretation built around it) is where psychological intervention lives — and where the Tunnel is exited.

Module 05
Module 05 · Pain neuroscience

Pain neuroscience. Understanding visceral sensitivity.

Central sensitisation and visceral hypersensitivity are real neurobiological processes. Pain neuroscience education that changes how the brain processes pain by changing its understanding of pain.

Pain is an output

Not only a damage signal

The brain produces pain.
When the brain reads threat, it amplifies pain signals. When the brain reads safety, it down-regulates them. This is not 'pain is in your head' — the pain is real. It is produced by a nervous system that has become sensitised.
Central sensitisation

The dial has been turned up

Normal sensations are felt as painful.
The pain threshold lowers across the central nervous system. Sensations that wouldn't have registered before now register as painful. The gut is doing its job; the regulatory dial has been turned up.
Visceral hypersensitivity

The gut's specific case

Sensitivity, not damage.
In DGBI, visceral hypersensitivity means the gut's pain threshold is lower than baseline. Normal motility, normal gas, normal contractions are felt as painful. The investigations come back normal because the gut is normal — the sensitivity is the diagnosis.
Understanding is therapeutic

Knowing changes the signal

Different understanding → different processing.
The intervention isn't to override the pain or to ignore it. It's to understand it differently — which changes how the brain processes future signals. Understanding is itself therapeutic. This is the evidence base.

Module 06
Module 06 · Food, fear, function

Food, fear, and function. The psychology of eating with DGBI.

Food anxiety is among the most functionally impairing secondary consequences of DGBI. Restriction and avoidance become a layer of disability independent of the underlying condition. Not a nutrition module — a regulation module about food.

Threat pairing

Food paired with danger

Anticipatory anxiety rises before meals.
When eating produces symptoms, the Threat system pairs food with danger. The pairing is biological and automatic. Anticipatory anxiety rises before meals — and the anxiety itself contributes to the symptom that follows.
Restriction as safety

The trap of avoidance

Restriction reduces immediate threat, increases long-term load.
Restriction becomes safety-seeking — and the more the food list narrows, the higher the threat response to anything outside the list. Restriction reduces immediate anxiety but increases long-term constriction.
Graded exposure

Regulation-first reintroduction

Small. Predictable. Within regulation.
Graded exposure within a regulation-first framework. Not 'just eat it' — but deliberate, small, predictable reintroduction in a Soothe-accessible state. The body learns safety only when the system carrying it is safe enough to learn.
Function over food

What eating is for

Eating is participation, connection, energy.
When DGBI organises eating around symptom-avoidance, the function of food collapses. Eating becomes a clinical event, not a human one. Restoring function means restoring eating to its place in connection, energy, pleasure — not just symptom management.

Module 07
Module 07 · Values despite symptoms

Values despite symptoms. A life not organised around the gut.

The ultimate functional impairment of DGBI is not the symptom itself — it is the progressive organisation of life around avoiding symptoms. Cancelled plans, restricted activities, narrowed relationships, identity defined by illness rather than values.

Values Compass

What matters most

Direction, not destination.
The Values Compass identifies what matters most across life domains — relationships, work, health, creativity, contribution. Values are directions you move in, not destinations you arrive at. They remain available even in active symptoms.
Where illness narrowed

Map what was lost

Naming the cost makes recovery visible.
Naming what the illness has narrowed — the cancelled plans, the lost roles, the constricted relationships — is part of the work. Loss that isn't named cannot be recovered. The mapping is the precondition for the rebuilding.
Pacing principles

Neither avoidance nor over-effort

Reps the system can absorb.
Pacing is the middle path between avoidance (which compounds constriction) and over-effort (which compounds depletion). Reps the system can absorb — and recover from. The pace is set by the regulatory window, not by the goal.
Micro-autonomy recovery

Agency restored slowly

One micro-move at a time.
Agency is rebuilt through micro-moves — small deliberate actions in line with values, taken at the pace the system permits. The cumulative effect of small autonomies is the restoration of a life that is bigger than the illness.

Module 08
Module 08 · Integration

Integration. Your 12-week gut–brain plan.

The integrative final module. Daily practice, weekly reflection, flare protocol, and early warning list. The architecture for living the framework without ongoing clinical input.

Daily practice

Three minutes, twice a day

Reps the system can sustain.
Three physiological sighs in the morning. Three in the evening. The Centre work is daily, small, and non-negotiable. It is the maintenance dose — not the heroic intervention.
Weekly reflection

What worked, what tilted

Data, not judgement.
A short weekly review. What worked. What tilted you toward the Tunnel. What you learned. The reflection is short — five minutes — because long reflection becomes its own avoidance. Data, not judgement.
Flare protocol

What to do in an active flare

The pre-decided pathway.
The flare protocol is decided when you're regulated — not when you're in the flare. S.H.I.F.T.™ deployed early. Restriction limited. Plans not cancelled by default. Soothe inputs scheduled in, not after. A pre-decided pathway you can follow when the system narrows.
Early warning list

The signals that come first

Catch it at Stage 01.
The list of signals that come before the Tunnel closes — yours specifically. Sleep changes. Specific thoughts. Specific behaviours. Catching it at Instability is the work. The earlier the intervention, the smaller the cost.
