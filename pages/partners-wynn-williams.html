Welcome — how this programme works at Wynn Williams
For Wynn Williams partners, lawyers, and support staff

The framework,
calibrated to your practice.

This programme adapts the Executive Frame™ to the specific load of legal practice — the regulatory, cognitive, and decisional pressure that builds across long matters, adversarial environments, and the work of holding clients through some of the hardest moments of their lives.

The work isn't about doing more. It's about noticing the architecture you're already running — and refining it so the long career holds. Honest conversations, constructive negotiations — including the ones with yourself.

What it is

Six structured modules

Each module is 60–75 minutes, with workbook materials, a recorded session, and the relevant S.H.I.F.T.™ tools. Available across the programme period.

How it's delivered

Cohort + self-paced

Live sessions for cohort discussion and practice. Self-paced module review for the time pressures of practice. Confidential individual sessions available alongside.

What it's not

Not performance management

Engagement is voluntary, attendance isn't reported to the firm, and individual sessions are confidential. The work is yours.

Programme overview — six modules · the architecture for sustained legal practice
Calibrated for Wynn Williams

Your programme,
at a glance.

The modules below cover the architecture of the programme delivered to Wynn Williams. Each module includes workbook materials, a recorded session, and the relevant S.H.I.F.T.™ tools. Module 05 is offered as optional and is particularly relevant for family-law and dispute-resolution practitioners.

01

The Tunnel — recognising the sequence in legal practice

How the regulatory, cognitive, and decisional systems narrow under sustained adversarial pressure — across litigation, advisory work, and partner-track demands. The pattern, the mechanism, and the early signals before the second cup of coffee at 4pm.

60 min · workbook
02

Centre — regulation under courtroom and client load

Physiological tools calibrated for hearings, mediations, and high-stakes client meetings. The S.H.I.F.T.™ Method as a portable practice — usable between matters, before submissions, and in the corridor before walking back in.

75 min · workbook
03

Clarify — fact versus narrative under load

Cognitive tools for separating signal from story when working memory is depleted and case files are stacking. Particularly relevant to high-volume case work, complex litigation, and the cognitive cost of holding multiple matters concurrently.

75 min · workbook
04

Choose — values-aligned action in adversarial contexts

Practising agency in environments designed to provoke reaction. Implementation intentions for the courtroom and the email inbox at 11pm. The architecture of professional discipline — including saying no clearly, recommending honestly, and protecting the long arc of practice.

75 min · workbook
05

Vicarious load — the cost of carrying clients' weight

Especially relevant for family law, dispute resolution, and any practice involving sustained client distress. Recognising and metabolising the regulatory residue of the work — without rationalising it as "just part of the job." Drawing on vicarious-trauma research and clinical experience.

60 min · optional · workbook
06

Architecture for a 30-year career

The habits and patterns that hold a long legal career together. Recovery as a clinical skill, not an indulgence. Identity beyond the practising certificate. Closing module with a personal architecture review and forward plan.

60 min · workbook
How the programme runs — cadence, format, and confidentiality
Built around the realities of legal practice

The shape
of the work.

Cohort cadence

Six modules over six to eight weeks

Live cohort sessions delivered fortnightly across the firm's offices (Auckland, Christchurch, Queenstown) and via secure video link. Workshops are paced to fit alongside practice — never on top of it. Recordings available for those who can't attend live.

Workbook + practice

Tools, not theory

Each module includes a workbook with structured exercises designed to be done between sessions. The work happens on the page and in the practice — not just in the room. Time investment is around 30 minutes per module beyond the live session.

Confidentiality

Individual sessions are confidential

Staff may book one-to-one psychology sessions as part of the programme. These are confidential by professional standard — sessions, content, and attendance are not shared with the firm. The firm sees aggregate engagement only.

CPD-aligned

NZLS CPD applicable

The programme content is aligned with the New Zealand Law Society's CPD competency areas around practice management, client care, and professional responsibility. CPD logging templates available on request.

Common questions — from Wynn Williams staff
Questions, answered briefly

Frequently
asked.

Is attendance reported to the firm? T

The firm receives aggregate engagement data only — total module completions and overall satisfaction. Individual attendance, content of sessions, and one-to-one work are not shared with the firm. The work is yours.

How much time does the programme actually take? T

Each module is 60–75 minutes for the live session, plus around 30 minutes of workbook practice between sessions. Across six to eight weeks, that's roughly 7–9 hours total. Recorded sessions are available for those weeks where practice gets in the way.

I'm a partner — is this designed for me? T

Yes. Partners often carry the most sustained regulatory load — client management, team leadership, business development, and the matter work all running concurrently. The programme is calibrated for senior practitioners as well as junior staff. Several modules (particularly Module 06) are specifically oriented to long-career architecture.

I'm in family law and the vicarious load module sounds confronting. Is it safe to engage with? T

Module 05 is offered as optional precisely because of this. It is built on vicarious-trauma research and clinical experience, paced carefully, and supported by individual sessions if needed. It is also one of the modules most consistently described by family-law practitioners as the one they most needed.

Can I access the programme content after it ends? T

Yes. The recordings, workbooks, and resources remain available to participating staff for two years after the programme period closes. The S.H.I.F.T.™ Method, the C³ Loop, and the architecture stay with you regardless.

What if I want to leave the programme partway through? T

That's fine. Engagement is voluntary throughout. If you want to step out for a period and return later, the cohort structure allows for that. If you want to step out entirely, no notice is required — the resources you've accessed remain available to you.
