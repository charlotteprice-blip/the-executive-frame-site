What DGBI is · what it is not

Disorders of gut–brain interaction
are real, structurally specific.

DGBIs — including IBS, functional dyspepsia, and related conditions — are not "all in your head." They are conditions where the gut–brain regulatory system has become over-tuned to threat. The science is well-established. The intervention is structured.

DGBIs are not a sign of structural damage. They are a sign that the gut–brain system has become over-tuned to threat — detecting more, predicting more, and amplifying more than the situation calls for. This programme teaches you to work with that system, not against it.

This programme is designed to complement medical care — not to replace it. Continue your existing medical management. Psychological intervention treats the regulatory system that drives gut dysfunction. It does not diagnose disease.
