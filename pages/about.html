02 — Why Executive Frame

The framework was not designed in a classroom.

It grew out of years of working alongside people carrying significant responsibility — people who were capable, committed, and doing their best, yet finding it harder to hold everything under sustained pressure. It speaks to that reality: where the strain begins, why it happens, and what is needed to support people more effectively within it.

01 A system for real life — not ideal conditions +

The Executive Frame™ is a human performance system for real life — not ideal conditions.

It is for the moments when things feel just slightly too much. When you are coping, but only just. When thinking feels harder, options feel fewer, and everything starts to blur together.

That experience shows up everywhere. For a parent holding multiple demands at once. For a leader carrying decisions that do not have clear answers. For professionals managing pressure, pace, and expectation.

Different roles. Same human system. At some point, everyone enters that space — where it becomes harder to think clearly, stay steady, and act with intention.

The Executive Frame™ meets people there. Not when everything is calm and controlled — but in the middle of it, when capacity is stretched and support is needed most.

It provides a way to steady, to think, and to move forward again — even when things feel tight, noisy, or stuck.

02 Why existing frameworks did not always translate +

In practice, many of the frameworks I was trained in were sound — but difficult to carry into real-world pressure.

They worked well in structured environments, where there was space to think, reflect, and apply them deliberately. But outside of that — in busy offices, complex systems, or everyday life — they often became harder to access.

Not because people were unwilling or unmotivated, but because pressure changes what is available.

Under load, cognitive capacity narrows. The very skills these frameworks rely on — reflection, discipline, emotional regulation — are the first to become less accessible.

What became clear over time was that people did not need more theory. They needed something usable in the moment.

This led to an integration of multiple approaches — drawing on established psychological models, but translating them into practical, real-time tools. Tools that can be used in everyday situations. Tools that do not rely on ideal conditions. Tools that I have found myself returning to, again and again, in the same moments others describe.

The aim was not to simplify psychology, but to make it more accessible — something that can travel with people, not stay behind in the room it was taught in.

03 What Executive Frame™ was built to do +

The Executive Frame™ brings together three complementary areas of psychological science — emotional regulation, executive functioning, and self-determination — into a single, applied architecture.

Rather than sitting as separate models, they are organised in a sequence that reflects how people actually function under pressure, and are designed to be used in real time, not only in reflection.

The C³ Loop™ describes how these capacities interact moment to moment, while S.H.I.F.T.™ provides a practical way to work with them when pressure is immediate.

03 — How Sarah works

Clinically grounded. Systems-aware.

Sarah’s practice is built around the populations she has spent her career studying. Each client is met where they are — clinically, professionally, and biologically — and the work is calibrated to that specific context.

For individuals, the work is depth-clinical: assessment, formulation, intervention. For organisations, it is systems-level: structural patterns, leadership architecture, the regulatory climate of teams and practices. For families, it sits between the two — addressing the system that holds individual symptoms in place.

Across all three, the framework is the same. Centre. Clarify. Choose. The depth of application is what calibrates.

Programmes & co-founded work

Studio Mindspace. An ADHD intervention framework.

Sarah is the co-founder of Studio Mindspace, a specialised intervention framework for the assessment and treatment of ADHD in adults. The programme is grounded in Russell Barkley’s executive functioning model and integrates science-based strategies addressing cognitive, emotional, and behavioural regulation.

The work sits alongside Executive Frame™ as a parallel clinical offering — specifically designed for the population whose pressure under load has a neurodevelopmental signature.
