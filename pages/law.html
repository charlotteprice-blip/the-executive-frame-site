The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Emotional regulation before everything else. You cannot think clearly from inside threat activation — and adversarial work is sustained threat activation by design. The physiological foundation without which legal reasoning cannot run cleanly.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Strategic thinking under pressure. Legal reasoning protected from reactivity — the difference between strategic advocacy and reactive defence. Working memory, inhibition, cognitive flexibility under contested-matter load.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Professional authorship. Autonomy within legal systems. Competence grounded in evidence, not validation. Relatedness inside an adversarial profession. A career chosen, not survived.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because you cannot think clearly from inside threat activation. Clarify must precede Choose because you cannot act with intention from cognitive overwhelm. This is biology — and adversarial practice runs on the same biology as any other human work.
