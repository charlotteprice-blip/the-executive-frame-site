The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Bruce Perry's neurosequential principle. A dysregulated nervous system cannot access cortical function. The family law practitioner who enters a meeting already activated cannot read the client clearly — and the failure is entering without noticing. The Center domain addresses this structurally.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Barkley's executive function research and Beck's cognitive model. Metacognition under load. Recognising cognitive distortions — confirmation bias, rescue fantasy, catastrophising — in real-time legal reasoning. Cognitive restructuring as a trainable professional skill.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Deci & Ryan's Self-Determination Theory. Dweck's growth orientation. McGonigal's stress-mindset research. Boundary architecture across the matter. Sustainable motivational design across a family law career — not extracted, built.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence reflects clinical reality, not preference. The brain operates bottom-up. Subcortical regulatory systems must be stabilised before cortical functions can run cleanly. Attempting to think through a difficult family law situation while dysregulated is not impaired thinking — it is thinking cut off from its regulatory substrate. The architecture addresses this structurally.

Cognition Under Load

"Capacity drops. Confidence doesn't."
Eight weeks after Module 1. The C³ Loop introduced as steady-state operational tool. Barkley's executive functions applied to family law reasoning. Agency & Sustainable Practice
