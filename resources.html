Questions, answered

Quick answers.

01How are resources delivered?

Digital tools arrive by email within five minutes, as clean, printable PDFs. Card sets ship within New Zealand, international on request. If anything hasn't arrived within the hour, email admin@sarahanticich.co.nz — we'll sort it quickly.

02Who are these resources for?

Anyone working under pressure — and the people who support them. Choose the context that matches yours; the framework underneath is identical, so nothing is wasted if your context changes. Health-related materials are educational and supportive — they sit alongside, never instead of, care from your GP, specialist, or psychologist.

03Can I use the materials professionally?

A personal purchase covers your own use. The Facilitator Toolkit includes a licence for use with your own clients, patients, or groups; wider distribution across clinics, firms, and organisations is covered by organisational licensing — talk to us.
