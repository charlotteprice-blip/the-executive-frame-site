The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Emotional regulation before everything else — built into your week, when you can take it. Same biology. Same architecture. Different format for different lives. The physiological foundation that makes clinical clarity possible.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Structured thinking under pressure — taught in modules you can return to. Working memory, inhibition, cognitive flexibility. Decision quality preserved when capacity compresses. Available whenever you have the bandwidth.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Professional authorship — built across the modules as you work through them. Autonomy in your clinical practice. Competence grounded in evidence. Connection with a clinician community.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable, regardless of format. Center must precede Clarify because you cannot think clearly from inside threat activation. Clarify must precede Choose because you cannot act with intention from cognitive overwhelm. This is biology — and it holds whether the format is live or self-paced.
