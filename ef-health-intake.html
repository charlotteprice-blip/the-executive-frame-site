Gut-Brain Intake Form

A psychologically informed intake for gut-brain health support

This form helps your clinician understand you as a whole person — your symptoms, how you have been feeling, and what matters most to you right now. It takes approximately 15–20 minutes to complete.

There are no right or wrong answers. Please respond based on your experience over the past two weeks.

Privacy: Your responses are confidential and will only be accessed by your treating clinician at MacMurray Clinic. This form is not monitored in real time. If you are in crisis, call 111, text or call 1737 (free, 24/7), or go to your nearest emergency department.
