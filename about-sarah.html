About Sarah

The clinician behind the architecture.

Dr Sarah Anticich is a Clinical and Industrial/Organisational Psychologist, and the founder of Executive Frame™. She built the framework over twenty years of clinical practice with the people most affected by sustained pressure — senior counsel, executives, clinicians, families, and patients living with chronic conditions.

02 — Programmes

Three programmes where the work compounds.

i.
Studio Mindspace
Co-founded · adult ADHD intervention
A specialised intervention framework for the assessment and treatment of ADHD in adults. Grounded in Russell Barkley’s executive functioning model, it integrates science-based strategies to address cognitive, emotional, and behavioural regulation. The applied foundation under the EF|Neurodiversity arm.
ii.
MediMinds
Clinical · disorders of gut–brain interaction
Integrated psychological care for Disorders of Gut–Brain Interaction (DGBIs). Developed alongside gastroenterology partners and refined through sustained patient work — the clinical pathway that sits under EF|Health — DGBI.
iii.
MacMurray Centre
Partnership · gastroenterology & endoscopy
Affiliation with the MacMurray Centre, New Zealand’s leading digestive health facility. Evidence-based, team-based care across an integrated DGBI clinical pathway — the model for what cross-disciplinary partnership looks like in practice.
