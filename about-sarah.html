About · Dr Sarah Anticich

The person behind the practice.

Dr Sarah Anticich is a Clinical and Industrial/Organisational Psychologist with more than twenty years of experience across the public and private sectors of New Zealand and Australia.

Academic background

Grounded in the science.

Dr Anticich holds a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Psychology from the University of Queensland, where her doctoral research focused on the prevention of anxiety and the development of emotional resilience in children and adolescents.

She also holds a Postgraduate Diploma in Clinical Psychology, a Master of Science (Industrial and Organisational Psychology) with First Class Honours, a Master of Science with First Class Honours, a Bachelor of Science (Psychology), and a First Class Honours degree in Child and Family Psychology endorsed in Education.

Clinical & sector experience

Where the work has lived.

Her academic and clinical training reflect a deep commitment to the integration of developmental, clinical, and organisational perspectives — supporting both individual change and systemic transformation. She brings this interdisciplinary foundation to therapy, supervision, leadership consulting, and programme development.

Her career spans healthcare, education, corrections, high-performance sport, and the legal and insurance industries. She has provided specialist psychological services in hospitals, schools, community health, and corporate consulting environments — offering assessment, intervention, supervision, and system-level advisory work.

Clinical focus

Assessment & treatment

Experienced in the assessment and treatment of trauma, emotional dysregulation, executive functioning difficulties, and identity disturbance. An approved ACC Sensitive Claims provider, supporting individuals through complex recovery and regulation processes.

Innovative programmes

Building what's missing

Co-founder of Studio Mindspace, a specialised framework for the assessment and treatment of ADHD in adults — grounded in Russell Barkley's executive functioning model — and founder of the Executive Frame™, applied across eight programme arms.

Philosophy & approach

Scientific integrity, human delivery.

Across all domains, the work is underpinned by a commitment to scientific integrity, client-centred practice, and real-world application — precision, warmth, and insight, whether with individual clients, interdisciplinary teams, or leadership groups navigating complexity and change.
