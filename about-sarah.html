About Sarah

The clinicianbehind the architecture.

Dr Sarah Anticich is a Clinical and Industrial/Organisational Psychologist, and the founder of Executive Frame™. She built the framework over twenty years of clinical practice with the people most affected by sustained pressure — senior counsel, executives, clinicians, families, and patients living with chronic conditions.

The practice

More about the work.

01Approach

Precision, warmth, and clarity — with individuals, teams, and leadership groups navigating change. The work is structural and diagnostic: it names what fails under pressure and builds architecture for each stage.

02Who Sarah works with

Legal professionals, corporate and executive populations, health professionals, families, and adults with ADHD and related executive-function profiles — across Auckland, Christchurch, Queenstown, and by telehealth.

03Recognition & partnerships

A recognised provider and CPD partner, including work with the New Zealand Law Society, Montrose Chambers, and the MacMurray Centre. ACC Sensitive Claims and Southern Cross provider.
