The Executive Frame™ Assessment.

Forty-eight statements across three validated frameworks — one integrated read of your emotional intelligence, executive functioning, and self-determination.

Rate each statement 1–5: 1 = Never · 5 = Almost always. Answer for the last few weeks, not your best week. There are no right answers — only honest ones.

The model: Emotional Intelligence (Goleman's five domains) maps to Centre · Executive Functioning (Barkley's model) maps to Clarify · Self-Determination (Deci & Ryan) maps to Choose. Together they produce your Executive Frame™ Performance Profile.

The Executive Frame™ Assessment is an educational self-reflection grounded in Russell Barkley's model of executive functioning, Daniel Goleman's emotional intelligence framework, and Deci & Ryan's Self-Determination Theory. It is not a diagnostic instrument, an ADHD screen, or a substitute for clinical assessment. Items 13–15 of the Self-Determination domain are reverse-scored. If anything here raises concern for you, your GP or a registered psychologist is a good first step — in a crisis dial 111 (NZ) / 000 (AU).
