Understanding first

Understanding first. Then practical support.

EF|Neurodiversity starts from difference, not deficit. The work begins with understanding — how your attention, energy, sensory system, and executive functioning actually operate — and what that makes easy, and what it makes costly.

From there, support is practical: recognising strengths and building on them, reducing the friction that doesn't need to be there, and designing ways of living, working, parenting, and relating that hold up in real life — not in an ideal week.

The Executive Frame™ approach

Centre → Clarify → Choose, calibrated to your wiring.

Centre

Make space for steadiness

Sensory and nervous-system steadiness comes first — not as a nicety, but because everything else gets easier from there.

Clarify

Get the plan out of your head

Reduce executive-function load by moving plans, reminders, and decisions onto structures that hold them for you.

Choose

One realistic next step

Sized to your real capacity today — not the capacity you wish you had, or the one someone else expects.

Good to know

Quick answers.

01Who is EF|Neurodiversity for?

Neurodivergent adults, parents, and families — including people exploring whether ADHD or autism might explain long-standing patterns. You don't need a diagnosis to begin, and support is shaped to your wiring rather than against it. If you're not sure where to start, the Capacity Check™ will point you to a sensible first step.

02What is Parenting in the Wild?

Chaos to Clarity — strengths-based group support for parents navigating neurodivergence, big feelings, competing needs, and real family life. It's practical and judgement-free, built for the family you actually have. Enquire to hear about upcoming groups.

03How is support delivered?

One-to-one, in small groups, or through parenting support — in person across Auckland, Christchurch, and Queenstown, or by telehealth nationwide. Begin with a short intake and we'll match you to the right format and pace.

04What will I work with?

Portable, real-time tools built around Centre → Clarify → Choose: the C³ Loop™, the S.H.I.F.T.™ reset, and printable supports from the Resources hub — plus strategies designed around your own patterns, so you leave each session with something you can use the next day.
