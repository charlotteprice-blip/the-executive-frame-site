The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Regulation that works with your nervous system, not against it. The neurodivergent system has its own signals — settle into how your system actually settles, not how you've been told it should. The physiological foundation under sustained ND load.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Executive function support calibrated to ND patterns. Working memory, inhibition, and cognitive flexibility tools that respect how your brain actually processes — not adapted from frameworks designed for neurotypical systems.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Choose to operate in ways that match how you're wired. Autonomy that respects your processing style. Competence grounded in your own evidence. Connection with people who can see your system clearly. A way of working that sustains across the long arc.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because the neurodivergent system can't think clearly through sustained dysregulation. Clarify must precede Choose because trying to redesign your operating system without naming what's loaded burns capacity you don't have. This is biology — calibrated to how your system actually works.
