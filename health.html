The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Emotional regulation before everything else. Access to the prefrontal cortex. The physiological foundation without which Clarity and Agency cannot be built. You cannot think clearly from inside threat activation — and sustained clinical exposure is repeated threat activation by design.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Structured thinking under pressure. Working memory, inhibition, cognitive flexibility, and planning under load. Diagnostic reasoning protected from fatigue. Decision quality preserved when capacity compresses. You cannot act with intention from cognitive overwhelm.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Professional authorship. Autonomy — genuine choice inside the constraints of clinical work. Competence — confidence grounded in evidence, not validation. Relatedness — the belonging that fuels purpose. A practice chosen, not survived.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because you cannot think clearly from inside threat activation. Clarify must precede Choose because you cannot act with intention from cognitive overwhelm. This is biology, not preference — and clinical practice runs on the same biology as any other human work.
