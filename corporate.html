The Executive Frame™
Three evidence bases. One biological sequence.
The Executive Frame™ is built on three converging sciences — each addressing a different layer of human performance. The C³ Loop is the operational mechanism that integrates all three into a single, repeating cycle.
Step One · Stability
Center
Emotional Intelligence
Emotional regulation before strategic decision-making. Decisions made from dysregulation produce dysregulation downstream. Settle the senior team before changing the system around them. The physiological substrate of sustainable leadership.
Step Two · Clarity
Clarify
Executive Functioning
Strategic thinking protected from reactivity. Separate the work from the conditions that make the work harder than it needs to be. Name the architectural issues. Surface the structural drivers of recurring symptoms.
Step Three · Agency
Choose
Self-Determination Theory
Lead from architecture, not reactivity. Build the conditions for sustained, values-aligned action across the organisation. Capacity built inside the system, not extracted from it.
S.H.F.T.™
The real-time micro-intervention. 60 seconds. Anywhere.
SSlow — interrupt the threat response
HHold — physiological sigh, regulate
FFocus — one next step
TTime — sequence, protect choice
+Plus — repair, reflect, integrate

The sequence is non-negotiable. Center must precede Clarify because strategic decisions made from sustained dysregulation produce more dysregulation downstream. Clarify must precede Choose because organisational architecture cannot be redesigned from cognitive overwhelm. This is biology — and organisations run on the same biology as any other human work.
