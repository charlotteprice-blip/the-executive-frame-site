Why · The Science

Three evidence bases. One sequence.

Not eclectic. Not opinion. The Executive Frame™ rests on three of the most robust evidence bases in applied psychology — emotional intelligence, executive functioning, and self-determination theory. Each domain anchored by a primary theorist; each enriched by complementary clinical traditions. Eight literatures that converge on the same architectural insight: regulate, then clarify, then choose.

The framework The method (S.H.I.F.T.™)
Centre Domain · Stability · Emotional Intelligence
The body settles before the mind can read.
The brain operates bottom-up. Subcortical regulatory systems must be stabilised before cortical functions can run cleanly. This is not a preference — it is how the nervous system is organised.
Primary Anchor Perry Neurosequential model ⌄
The brain is built and operates bottom-up. The brainstem and limbic system must be stabilised before cortical reasoning can engage. A dysregulated practitioner attempting to "think through" a difficult situation is using executive function cut off from its regulatory substrate.
Contributing Tradition Goleman Emotional intelligence ⌄
Operationalises regulation as four linked competencies — self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship management. Supplies the vocabulary for the interpersonal register: how dysregulation transmits across the room.
Contributing Tradition Linehan Dialectical behaviour therapy ⌄
The most highly evidenced skills-based approach to distress tolerance and emotion regulation in the clinical literature. DBT's core insight — that distress can be tolerated without being eliminated, and the capacity is trainable.
Clarify Domain · Cognition · Executive Functioning
The mind separates fact from interpretation.
Cognition under load is a trainable capacity. Executive functions can be strengthened through structured practice — and weakened by sustained load without recovery.
Primary Anchor Barkley Executive function research ⌄
Identifies inhibitory control, working memory, cognitive flexibility, and emotional self-regulation as the core executive functions — and demonstrates these capacities are not fixed traits. The C³ Loop is, in Barkley's terms, structured executive function activation.
Contributing Tradition Beck Cognitive therapy ⌄
The cognitive model identifies the automatic thoughts and systematic distortions — catastrophising, personalisation, mental filtering, all-or-nothing thinking — that compress judgment under emotional load.
Applied Lineage Ericsson · Beilock Performance psychology ⌄
Deliberate practice research (Ericsson) and the psychology of performance under pressure (Beilock) extend the Clarify domain into trainable skill. Cognition under load is a domain of expertise.
Choose Domain · Agency · Self-Determination Theory
Agency restored — not the perfect move, the deliberate one.
Three universal psychological needs — autonomy, competence, relatedness — sit beneath all sustainable motivation. Work that meets these needs produces durable engagement.
Primary Anchor Deci & Ryan Self-determination theory ⌄
SDT identifies autonomy, competence, and relatedness as the substrate of intrinsic motivation. The pattern of attrition seen across high-load professions is the predictable result of work that systematically frustrates these needs.
Contributing Tradition Dweck Growth orientation research ⌄
Identifies the difference between fixed and growth mindsets — and the clinical consequence of treating trainable capacities as stable identity traits. The reframe Dweck supplies is the precondition for the work that follows.
Contributing Tradition McGonigal Motivation & stress-mindset science ⌄
Synthesises motivation, willpower, and stress-mindset research. The core finding — that stress viewed as enhancing rather than threatening produces better performance and lower physiological cost.

These are not eight competing schools. They are eight literatures that converge on the same architectural insight: human performance under sustained load runs on a biological sequence — regulate, then clarify, then choose. The Executive Frame™ is the integration of that converging evidence into a single, teachable, repeatable framework.
